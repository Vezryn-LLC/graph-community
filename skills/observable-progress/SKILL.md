# Observable Progress

## Objective
Report meaningful evidence rather than performative activity.

## Practice
1. Differentiate launched, working, blocked, validated, published and completed.
2. Link actual commits, test outputs, PRs or issue updates.
3. Send coordinator meaningful completion/blocker events only.
4. Report UNKNOWN when evidence cannot establish the outcome.

Guidance only. Do not introduce new Graph services, approval gates or required reviewer loops.
