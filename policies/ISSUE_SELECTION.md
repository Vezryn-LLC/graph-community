# Selection, authority and dependencies

Read complete original issue bodies/comments and live labels/PRs. New assignments
must be open, `status:ready`, enrolled, actionable and unowned. Exclude blocked,
in-progress and awaiting-merge issues even if also marked ready. Enrollment limits
discovery, not credential permissions or active-worker termination. Do not invent
credentials, expand task scope or perform consequential destructive/production
actions without their specific authority.

Prioritize delivery value, urgency, dependencies and overlap. Read out-of-scope
repositories only for dependency context. Inspect all current blockers before
requeue. An optional body declaration between `<!-- graph-dependencies -->` and
`<!-- /graph-dependencies -->` contains `Blocked by <full issue URL>` lines, or `None`.
It expresses current intent; preserve historical evidence. Reconcile native
dependency discrepancies before clearing them. Invalid declarations, partial
reads and stale observations never mean no dependencies. Closed/not-planned,
draft or unmerged work alone is not accepted delivery. Requeue only after every
next-step gate is verified; preserve genuine user decisions and unenrolled gates.

Choose one useful available playbook and supported native model/effort. Read
literal selected guidance from effective paths. Do not reload disabled defaults;
explain and defer if essential missing guidance prevents safe work. A library
disable marker cannot erase higher-priority standing instructions.
